Kimchi

  1. 1 napa cabbage
  2. 1/4 cup salt
  3. 2 tbsp Korean chili powder
  4. 1 tbsp garlic, minced
  5. 2 tbsp fish sauce
  6. 1 tbsp ginger, minced

Instructions

  • Cut cabbage, rub with salt, let sit for 2 hours
  • Rinse and drain
  • Mix chili powder, garlic, ginger, and fish sauce
  • Rub onto cabbage and store in an airtight container for fermentation.
